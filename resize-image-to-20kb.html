Why Government Portals Demand 20KB Images

Learn about the strict limits enforced by SSC, UPSC, and Indian government servers.

SSC Signature (10 KB - 20 KB)

Staff Selection Commission (SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD) mandates signature images between 10 KB and 20 KB, with dimensions of 4.0 cm x 2.0 cm. Files over 20.0 KB are immediately blocked.

UPSC Signature (10 KB - 40 KB)

Union Public Service Commission requires signatures between 10 KB and 40 KB (commonly scaled around 20 KB) with dimensions of 1000 x 550 pixels to ensure clean document printing on admit cards.

PAN Card Portal (Under 20 KB)

NSDL and UTIITSL portals reject signature scans that exceed 20 KB or lack 200 DPI resolution. IMLResizer applies both 20KB and 200 DPI simultaneously.
