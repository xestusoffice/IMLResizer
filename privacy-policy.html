Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: Immediately

Our Core Privacy Promise: 100% Client-Side Processing

When you resize, crop, or compress images on IMLResizer, your files are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud storage. All image processing calculations happen locally within your device's web browser using standard HTML5 Canvas and Web Workers APIs. Your identity documents, personal photos, and signatures remain solely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional image converter portals that upload photos to server disks, IMLResizer has no server-side storage infrastructure for your media. We do not store, copy, analyze, or transmit your images, EXIF metadata, or handwritten signature scans.

2. Information We May Collect

When you voluntarily submit a message through our Contact / Feedback Form, we collect your name, email address, message category, and inquiry details to respond to your technical questions. This data is handled exclusively by our support desk at xestus.office@gmail.com.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense Advertising

IMLResizer uses third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Local Storage Usage

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your preferred UI theme (Dark Mode vs Light Mode) and recent support ticket reference numbers. No personally identifiable files or photos are written to storage.
