Official Passport Photo Dimensions & Guidelines

US Visa & Passport

Dimensions: 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm / 600 x 600 px). White background, eyes positioned between 1 1/8 and 1 3/8 inches from bottom edge.

Schengen Europe Visa

Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm (300 DPI). Light gray or white background. Face must take up 70% to 80% of photo height (32 - 36 mm).

India Passport & OCI

Dimensions: 35 x 45 mm or 2 x 2 inches (for OCI). White background, neutral facial expression, ears visible, no reflection on glasses.
