About IMLResizer

Empowering candidates, students, and professionals with instantaneous, privacy-respecting photo utilities.

Why We Built IMLResizer

Millions of candidates applying for government exams (SSC, UPSC, Banking, NEET, Railways) and international visas face rejected applications simply because their photo or signature exceeded an arbitrary limit by a few kilobytes or lacked correct DPI metadata. We created IMLResizer to solve this problem permanently with 100% precision.

The Privacy Revolution

Unlike other websites that upload personal photos, signatures, and ID scans to their servers, IMLResizer executes 100% of its algorithms directly inside your web browser. Nothing ever leaves your computer or smartphone.

About Xestus
